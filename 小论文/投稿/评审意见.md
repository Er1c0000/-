Comments for Revision

修订意见

The authors propose a sparse Bayesian learning (SBL) approach to jitter decomposition to address the problem that the frequency bias introduced by data transitions in PRBS signals propagates to the amplitudes of all components. A redundant dictionary whose frequency resolution can be designed independently is constructed, and its active columns are identified by the automatic relevance determination (ARD) mechanism, replacing FFT peak-picking. The paper is of research significance, but the following issues remain:

作者提出了一种基于稀疏贝叶斯学习（SBL）的抖动分解方法，用于解决 PRBS 信号中数据跳变引入的频率偏差会传递到各分量幅值估计中的问题。论文构建了一个频率分辨率可独立设计的冗余字典，并通过自动相关性确定（ARD）机制识别其中的活跃列，以替代 FFT 寻峰。该论文具有一定研究意义，但仍存在以下问题：

The FFT peak in LS-FFT is a coarse estimate, but in practical engineering the frequency accuracy can be improved by interpolation (e.g., parabolic interpolation) or non-uniform DFT. The paper only compares against the original LS-FFT and does not include more advanced frequency-domain correction methods, limiting the generality of its conclusions.

LS-FFT 中的 FFT 峰值只是一个粗略估计，但在实际工程中，可以通过插值方法（如抛物线插值）或非均匀 DFT 提高频率精度。论文仅与原始 LS-FFT 方法进行比较，未纳入更先进的频域校正方法，因此限制了结论的普适性。

In Table 1, the estimation error of ISI for both methods stabilises around 0.30 ps (independent of the PJ frequency). The authors attribute this to “finite-sample statistical accumulation”, but no theoretical analysis or verification experiment (e.g., whether increasing the sample size reduces the bias) is provided.

在表 1 中，两种方法的 ISI 估计误差均稳定在约 0.30 ps 左右（与 PJ 频率无关）。作者将其归因于“有限样本统计累积”，但未提供理论分析或验证实验，例如样本数量增加后该偏差是否会减小。

In actual high-speed links, multiple non-harmonically related periodic jitter sources (e.g., multiple clock crosstalk) may exist. The paper assumes that the number of simultaneously present PJ components is small (SBL sparsity), but the experiments inject only a single PJ frequency and do not test the identification and decomposition accuracy when two or more PJ frequencies coexist.

在实际高速链路中，可能存在多个非谐波关系的周期性抖动源，例如多个时钟串扰源。论文假设同时存在的 PJ 分量数量较少（即满足 SBL 稀疏性假设），但实验中只注入了单一 PJ 频率，未测试两个或更多 PJ 频率同时存在时的识别与分解精度。

The conclusion section should add corresponding data to support the final conclusions.

结论部分应补充相应数据，以支撑最终结论。

It is recommended to include more journal papers published in the last three years in the reference list.

建议在参考文献中增加近三年发表的期刊论文。
